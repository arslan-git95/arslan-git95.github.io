How I pick creators.

Three rules I use on every campaign.

Trust beats follower count.

I look for organic, engaged audiences that trust the creator. Trust drives sales. Follower count does not.

Can the audience buy?

I check that viewers can afford and reach the product, not only how many of them there are.

Right country, right language.

I match the audience to the brand's target geography, and I write to creators in their own language.

Every creator is tracked in one Google Sheets pipeline, from first email to closed deal, published video and sales.
